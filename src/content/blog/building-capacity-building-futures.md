title: "Building Capacity, Building Futures: How Drone Training Strengthens Indigenous Communities"
description: "How drone training builds skills, jobs, and data sovereignty in Indigenous communities across Canada."
date: "2024-11-14"
---

Aboriginal Training Services uses drone training to build skills, jobs, and data sovereignty in Indigenous communities across Canada.

The aim is local capacity: people who can fly, collect data, and decide how that information serves their own community.
