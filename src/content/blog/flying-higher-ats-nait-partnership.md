---
title: "Flying Higher: ATS and NAIT Partner to Bring Drone Training into Higher Education"
description: "How Aboriginal Training Services is helping NAIT students, including Indigenous learners, gain Transport Canada certification and hands-on drone experience."
date: "2025-11-13"
---

Aboriginal Training Services is working with NAIT to bring drone training into higher education.

The partnership helps NAIT students, including Indigenous learners, prepare for Transport Canada certification, get hands-on drone experience, and connect academic learning with work in the field.
